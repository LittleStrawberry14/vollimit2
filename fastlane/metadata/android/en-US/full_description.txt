Volume Limit is a lightweight, efficient Android utility designed to enforce a maximum media volume limit, ensuring a consistent and safe auditory experience. 

Features:
- Deterministic Volume Control: Dynamically intercepts and corrects any attempt to exceed the user-defined volume threshold.
- Resource Optimized: Built using an event-driven ContentObserver architecture.
- Persistent Foreground Service: Maintains service integrity without cluttering your notifications.
- Battery Optimization: Built-in logic to handle aggressive power-management systems.
- Localized: Full support for English and Arabic (RTL).

Privacy-First: No telemetry, no analytics, and no network permissions required.
